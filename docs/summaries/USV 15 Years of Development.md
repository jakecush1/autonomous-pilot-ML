## Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**
- A survey by an early USV developer, so it's the kind of source the manual asks for.
- Cited by Sarda et al., which ties it to the rest of the reading list.

**Weaknesses**
- Stops in 2008, so there's nothing on Saildrone (founded 2012), today's long-endurance USVs, or ML.
- It's a history of the *technology*. The history of the *problem* (keeping a vessel on station and deciding when a human needs to act) may need its own root.
